App Install & User Guide

Install Astro MAPs AI directly on your mobile or desktop home screen in 10 seconds and learn how to read intraday timing slots.

📱 Android (Google Chrome)

  1. Open astromapsai.com in Google Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots (⋮) in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
  4. Launch directly from your home screen in compact app mode.

🍎 iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open astromapsai.com in Apple Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon (□↑) at the bottom bar.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap Add in the top-right corner.

💻 Desktop / Laptop (Chrome / Edge)

  1. Open astromapsai.com in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the Install icon on the right side of the address bar.
  3. Click "Install" to launch Astro MAPs AI as a standalone window.

CYAN — PCB KILL-ZONE (INSTITUTIONAL FLOW): Institutional liquidity rotation windows ideal for cross-verifying price action on TradingView.
