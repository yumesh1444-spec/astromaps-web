Effort vs Luck: Cosmic Power Aur Samay Ki Sachai

Har insaan din-raat mehnat karta hai—effort lagata hai—phir bhi kabhi kaam banta hai aur kabhi achanak ruk jaata hai. Yahin se sawal uthta hai: kya sab kuch sirf effort hai ya luck aur cosmic power ka bhi ek nishchit khel hai?

1. Cosmic Energy Ki Presence (उपस्थिति)

Hamare aas-paas cosmic energy ki उपस्थिति ko samajhne ke liye kahin door jaane ki zaroorat nahi hai. Dekhiye kaise Sun energy plants ko jeevit rakhti hai, unki vriddhi aur rang-roop ko badalti hai, aur kaise solar system mein Sun ke saath Moon ki gati aur uske effects har cheez par asar daalte hain.

2. Unchanging Cosmic Clock & Seasons

Rituen (seasons) aati hain aur jaati hain, lekin iske peeche chalne waali cosmic clock bina badle apni niyamit gati se chalti rehti hai. Yeh ek purana, sthir pattern hai jo samay ke har daur mein kaam karta hai.

3. Birth Month & Luck Patterns

Isi cosmic flow ke kaaran specific birth months mein janme logon ke luck aur timing patterns mein bhi khaas fark dikhta hai—kisi ke liye kaunsa samay anukool banta hai aur kab rukawat aati hai, iski jhalak samay ke is chakra mein milti hai.

4. Full Moon & New Moon Chart Observations

Jab hum market charts ko Full Moon aur New Moon ki gatividhiyon ke saath dekhte hain, toh chart ki chaal aur move mein saaf badlav dikhta hai. Yeh observation dikhati hai ki market ki move aur cosmic power ke beech ek gehra sambandh hai.

5. Cosmic Action Aur Tested Market Results

Jab cosmic power ke actions ko real market ke tested results ke saath joda jaata hai—jaise maine September 2020 mein COVID ke dobara aane ki prediction ki thi—toh yeh baat aur spasht hoti hai ki samay ke sanket sirf kalpana nahi balki parikshan (tested results) par khare utarte hain.

6. World Ke Liye Open Concept

Is verified concept ko ab duniya ke saamne khula rakha ja raha hai taaki log andhe tukkon ke bajay samay aur cosmic power ke is sambandh ko samajh sakein.

Cosmic energy hi yeh bata sakti hai ki wealth rahegi ya nahi—duniya mein iske alawa koi dusra parameter nahi hai.
